Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

The short version: Branch Out doesn't collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app (what you've tried, your notes, and your photos) stays on your iPhone.

This policy explains how the Branch Out app for iPhone (“Branch Out,” “the app”), made by Winston's Apps (“we,” “us”), handles your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Branch Out does not collect, transmit, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. The app doesn't connect to the internet at all. It has no servers, no sign-in, and no third-party services or software development kits (SDKs) for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Which activities (“stars”) you've completed, and when.
  • Your answers to “Would you do it again?” and any notes you write.
  • Photos you choose to add to a star, and an optional photo of yourself for the center of your galaxy. They're resized and saved as small copies inside the app.
  • Your Up next list, the scene you picked for each star, and app settings such as sounds.

We can't see any of this, and it never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below).

Photos and camera

When you add a photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker, which only gives the app the specific photo you select, not your photo library. If you use the camera, iOS asks your permission first, and the app only uses the camera while you're taking that picture. Photos are stored only inside the app on your device.

Sharing

If you tap “Share this star,” the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes, and to whom, is entirely your choice (for example Messages, Instagram, or TikTok). Once shared, that content is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Home screen widget

The Branch Out widget shows a daily suggestion and your Up next list. To do that, the app passes a small list of activity names to the widget through a shared folder on your iPhone. This never leaves your device.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Branch Out's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That's handled by Apple and controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Deleting your data

You're always in control. In the app, go to Timeline → ••• → Reset all progress to erase your completions, notes, and photos. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all of its data.

Children

Branch Out doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Because the app doesn't collect data, we don't expect it to change much.
